# Objective

To understand the basic concepts of graph data structures.
To represent museum rooms as vertices and doorways as edges.
To implement a graph using an adjacency matrix.
To accept room names and doorway details from the user.
To display the adjacency matrix representing room connectivity.
To identify and display the rooms directly connected to a selected room.
To insert a new vertex and its associated edges into the graph.
To remove a selected edge and update the graph representation.
To calculate the degree of a selected vertex and display its direct neighbours.
To understand graph insertion, deletion, and adjacency matrix operations using C++.
